GPS & setup

Open the hosted HTTPS page and allow location. If the car browser blocks GPS, use phone GPS and play audio through the car.

Browser GPS and background support need testing on your ATTO 1. This page does not run as a CarPlay or Android Auto app.

GPS uses device speed first, then a filtered position estimate. Speed is approximate; it cannot improve the GPS hardware. If fixes stop, revs ease to idle. Location stays on this device.

Location stays on this device.
Controls & credits

Hold the pad to rev. Slide down to soften throttle. Space / W: rev. ↑ / E and ↓ / Q: shift. Esc: stop. Manual shifts switch off auto shift.

Rev mode reaches the bouncing limiter. Drive mode simulates acceleration. GPS follows measured speed.
